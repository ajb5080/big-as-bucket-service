Dirt Work & Grading in Starkville

Mississippi clay settles, shifts, and creates drainage problems. We grade your yard to move water away from your foundation, fill low spots, and create a level surface. Full acre or a spot behind the garage — no job is too small. Land clearing: $500–$5,000+ per acre.

Grading & Lot Leveling

We grade your yard to improve drainage, fill low spots, and create a level surface for sod, seed, or a new patio. Full acre or a spot behind the garage — we do it all.

Best for: Drainage problems, low spots, foundation grading

Fill Dirt Delivery

Clean fill dirt delivered by the truckload. No rocks, no trash, no surprises. Perfect for raising low areas, filling ditches, or building up garden beds.

$150–$600 per truckload depending on distance

Topsoil Delivery

Screened topsoil ready for seed, sod, or plants. We'll help you figure out how much you need so you don't over-order.

$150–$600 per truckload

Driveway Grading

We'll reshape your gravel driveway, fill the ruts, add gravel if needed, and crown it so water runs off instead of pooling. Save yourself the bouncing and the mud.

$300–$1,500 depending on length and condition

Erosion Control

Heavy rain + Mississippi clay = erosion. We fix it with grading, drainage solutions, and strategic fill placement. Stop losing your yard one storm at a time.

Best for: Gully erosion, hillside washouts, drainage ditches
