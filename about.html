Meet Big A

The Guy Behind the Bucket

"Big A" is Brian Walker — a Starkville, MS local who got tired of watching homeowners juggle three contractors to get basic property work done. Tree guy for the dead oak. Dirt guy to fill the hole. Landscaper to finish the yard. Three calls, three schedules, three invoices.

Brian's solution: one crew handles it all. Trees, dirt, landscaping. You call once, we show up with the right equipment, and we don't leave until your property looks right. No passing the buck. No "that's not my department." Serving Oktibbeha County and surrounding areas.

The Tool That Sets Us Apart

The Bucket Truck Story

Most tree services only climb. Climbing is fine for small trees, but some are too big or dangerous to climb safely.

Big A's 60-foot bucket truck reaches trees climbers can't — towering oaks over houses, hickeys in power lines, pines leaning toward a shed. Speed, reach, and safety that climbing alone can't match.

And when the bucket can't reach (tight backyards, no gate access), our climbers take over. We're the only company in Starkville with both. Any tree, any size, any location.
